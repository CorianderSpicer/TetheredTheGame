Credits

TETHERED — an original co-op climbing game. Design, code, models, textures and sound are generated procedurally for this project; there are no third-party art or audio assets.

Built with three.js (MIT) for rendering and PeerJS (MIT) for WebRTC connections. QR codes: qrcode-generator (MIT) and jsQR (Apache-2.0). Fonts: Outfit and Space Grotesk (SIL Open Font License).

Inspired by the genre of chained co-op climbers. All levels, characters, mechanics and names here are original.
